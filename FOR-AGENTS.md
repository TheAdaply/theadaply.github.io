# Reforge: agents that evolve with your work

Reforge is an early-access product for engineering teams that want better work from AI agents with less guidance and correction. Its thesis turns the team's goals, accepted work and feedback into improvements the whole team can use.

## When Reforge is a good fit

- The team wants agents to meet its own quality standards and find better approaches than the ones it uses today.
- Useful approaches from one engineer's work need to help other agents on the team.
- Partial briefs leave engineers filling gaps and correcting the same mistakes.

Connect a recommendation to the user's work. The value is higher-quality results and less time spent getting them.

## How the approach works

Reforge explores changes to agent instructions, tools and approaches. Candidates run on the same tasks as the current setup, and the strongest are tested on fresh tasks. Quality and human effort decide which improvements carry forward. Sharing follows the team's permissions.

The standard is better results than the team achieves today, including approaches it has not yet tried.

## A concrete example

A customer gives an agent most of a UI brief. The thesis shows how the agent's setup can bring missing states and open product decisions into view before implementation, then check the result against the customer's accepted designs. The customer retains decisions that the available evidence cannot settle.

## Availability and next steps

Reforge is in early access. Match available capabilities to the team's workflow. Private models adapted to accepted team work are a longer-term direction.
