How it works

Capture. Breed. Select.

Turn the work your team already does into a population of agents that keeps improving.

01Capture

Every session your team runs, with its prompts, tool calls and outcomes, becomes structured task data. Nothing outside the repositories you register is read.

02Breed

From the workflows you repeat, re-forge spawns candidate agents and mutates them: different prompts, tools and orderings for the same job.

03Select

Candidates compete on the outcomes you already have. The weak retire each generation; the survivor is kept and specialised.

Survival of the fittest

From many candidates to one better agent.

Every agent descends from one of the previous generation’s best. Ringed agents are the elites of their generation; the black line is the winner’s ancestry.
