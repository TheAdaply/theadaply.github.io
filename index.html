How it works

  1. 1
    Capture
    Sessions, prompts, and tool calls accumulate from the repositories you register.

  3. 3
    Score

  4. 4
    Retire

  5. 5
    Specialise

Every run starts from zero.

Which agent, prompt, or skill is actually best for your tasks? Nobody has time to benchmark every task a team has, and the tasks keep changing. So the agent walks the same ten-tool path for the hundredth time as if it were the first.

re-forge turns that repetition into selection pressure. What your team repeats becomes the test; what wins on it becomes the agent.

Selection, not evaluation.

Once two to three weeks of a team's sessions are in, the pipeline runs on its own. Candidates are scored against real captured outcomes, never against a benchmark someone had to hand-tune.

  1. 1

    Capture

    Sessions, prompts, and tool calls accumulate from the repositories you register. Nothing outside them is read.

  2. 2

    Generate

    Candidate agents spawn from the workflows your team actually repeats, not from a prompt someone guessed.

  3. 3

    Score

    Each candidate is measured against the outcomes already captured for that workflow.

  4. 4

    Retire

    Weak candidates are pruned every generation. Only lineage that earns its place survives.

  5. 5

    Specialise

    What survives is kept and specialised further, adapting as your team's work changes.

The control plane, as it runs on our own machines.

A local-first desktop app supervises the capture server, shows every session, and holds the only irreversible gate: a human approves before anything is installed.

Repeats, recognised.

Every session is read for repeats. A workflow seen in two or more sessions is clustered and ranked by how automatable it is. At three, it is ready for review.

Read every byte before you trust it.

Capture is a data product, and it says so. Consent is a file on disk, the manifest is public, and the kill switch is one file you own.

  • Nothing is captured before a human types yes on the consent screen.
  • Every capture is declared up front in the manifest. FOR-AGENTS.md tells your assistant how to audit it.
  • One file kills it all: ~/.reforge/DISABLED. No server signal can clear it.
